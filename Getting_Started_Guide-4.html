The Sequence That Works

Follow this sequence to build the right foundation before adding complexity. Click any button to open that file immediately.

1
Education Layer · Day 1

Read the Science First

Understand how GLP-1 agonists suppress appetite, regulate blood sugar, and reduce inflammation — so every decision after this is grounded in understanding, not guesswork. Estimated read: 15–20 min.

2
Orientation · Day 1–2

Complete Your Onboarding Quiz

Take the 5-question profile quiz. It identifies your starting profile — Beginner, Optimiser, Inflammation Focus, or Peptide Explorer — and generates a personalised action plan through this entire suite.

3
Peptide Knowledge · Day 2–3

Study the Peptide Guide

Read the full Peptide Guide before selecting a stack. This is your reference for what each peptide does, how it differs, and which ones target fat loss vs muscle preservation vs recovery.

4
Dosing Precision · Before First Injection

Dosing Guide + Calculator — Together

The guide tells you what and when. The calculator tells you exactly how many units to draw. Use both every single time you prepare a new vial. There is no shortcut here.

5
Nutrition Setup · Week 1

Build Your Eating Foundation

Run your macro calculator, build your first week of meals, and generate a shopping list. Nutrition is the single biggest lever outside your peptides — don't leave it to chance or intuition.

6
Lifestyle Protocol · Week 1

Add the Training Layer

Pick your workout level, set your weekly schedule, and understand how sleep quality and stress management directly affect how well your peptides perform.

7
Advanced Protocols · Week 3–4

Layer In Stacking, Tools & Tracking

After 2–4 weeks on protocol, open Part II Protocols for stacking guidance, and the interactive tools for side effect tracking, progress charts, injection site rotation, and stack building.

⚠ Medical Disclaimer

All content in these files is for educational and informational purposes only. It does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Peptides and GLP-1 medications are prescription compounds or research chemicals depending on jurisdiction. Consult a licensed medical provider before beginning any injectable protocol, changing your diet, or starting new supplements. Do not self-prescribe based on any material in this suite.

Reconstitution & Storage: Always use bacteriostatic water. Store reconstituted peptides refrigerated at 2–8°C, protected from light. Swirl gently — never shake. Label every vial with the reconstitution date. Discard within the stability window noted in your Dosing Guide. If a vial appears cloudy, discoloured, or contains visible particles — discard immediately. Do not inject.
